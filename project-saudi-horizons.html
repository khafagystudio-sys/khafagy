( OVERVIEW )

Heritage and the modern city.

A poster series for the 95th Saudi National Day (2025 · 1446). Each poster pairs the old and the new: mud-brick forts, palms, camels, a falcon and a lantern next to the Riyadh skyline. A man looks out from a sandstone arch at the city ahead. A child sees his future self in a golden mirror. A woman carries the flag with a clay jar on her head. A boy holds a lantern over the skyline. The wide artwork at the top is the opening visual of the series.
